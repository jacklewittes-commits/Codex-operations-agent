## File naming
Set the sheet title to `aa-bb.mm_plan`:

- `aa`: two-digit start day of month
- `bb`: two-digit end day of month
- `mm`: two-digit month

Examples:
- `01-03.06_plan`
- `28-30.04_plan`

Keep site and experiment detail in `weekLabel` and inside the sheet, not in the file title.

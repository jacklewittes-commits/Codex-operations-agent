# PM Review Items

Use these detailed prompts when summarizing what the PM should review before publishing.

## Airstrip
- Flight day/time is coordinated with יונתן.
- Other people at the airstrip are identified.
- Workers have safety briefing and movement-control notes.
- External companies have exposure, frequency, communication, and interference flags.
- After-dark departure has a WhatsApp task for שמעון.

## Fuel
- `דס״ל` quantity covers the flight profile.
- Jet-fuel tank truck identity, starting liters, and 320-liter capacity assumption are correct.
- Refill drives appear in `Vehicles/Drives` with route, timing, truck, and driver/commander.
- Black jerrycans are loaded when tarmac refueling may happen away from the truck.
- `שדה תימן` refueling is coordinated with סתיו when needed.
- Oil mixing at 1:20 and uniform blending are captured when refueling is planned.

## Vehicles, Rooms, And Rentals
- Every person has outbound and return transport.
- Trailer vehicles have licensed commanders.
- Rental drivers are allowed, pickup hub is correct, כביש 6 dates are tracked, and mileage limits are checked.
- Every overnight attendee has a room for every night.
- Room capacity and gender separation are respected.
- צימר room turnover has a linen replacement note.

## Equipment, Trucking, And Food
- Critical equipment is listed and assigned to loading vehicles where known.
- Time-critical equipment arrival is cross-checked against driver and expected arrival.
- Truck load shapes match platform/asset and trailer counts.
- Food orders match headcount, special counts, vendors, and order channels.

## PM Checklist
- The tab includes open pre-experiment and during-experiment tasks.
- Blocking tasks are marked clearly.
- `Done` is used only for PM-confirmed completion.

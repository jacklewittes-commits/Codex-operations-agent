# PM Checklist Tab

The `PM Checklist` tab turns plan logic into PM-owned tasks. It is generated from the plan, assumptions, and flags; the PM should not invent rows manually.

## Columns
- Category
- Timing
- Task
- Owner
- Status
- Due / trigger
- Related plan area
- Notes
- Blocking?

## Status Rules
- Use `Open` for tasks still needing action.
- Use `Verify` for tasks that may already be handled but need PM confirmation.
- Use `Done` only when the PM explicitly provided completion evidence.
- Leave owner or due date blank, or mark as a review flag, when unknown.

## Timing Values
Use operational timing: pre-experiment, departure/loading, daily, during experiment, return, and post-return.

## Categories
Use PM-natural categories: airstrip, fuel, staffing/TRR, vehicles/rentals, lodging, equipment/loading, trucking, food, safety, vendor/contact, and publishing.

## Row Generation
Generate rows for tasks such as:

- Coordinate airstrip day/time with יונתן.
- Identify other people at the airstrip and manage safety/exposure risks.
- Send שמעון planned departure time when leaving after dark.
- Confirm jet-fuel tank truck identity, starting liters, and 320-liter capacity assumption.
- Plan any `שדה תימן` refill drive with the jet-fuel tank truck.
- Load black jerrycans for tarmac refueling away from the truck.
- Capture `דס״ל` oil mixing at 1:20 and uniform blending.
- Track rental pickup, return, כביש 6 add/remove, and mileage-limit checks.
- Notify צימר owner about room turnover and linen replacement.
- Cross-check critical equipment, truck load shape, food orders, and publishing approval.

Promote checklist items that can block safety, coordination, fuel, lodging, vehicles, or legal readiness into PM-facing unresolved flags.

# PM Operational Domains

Use this as internal planning context. It improves assumptions, flags, and checklist rows; it is not a separate PM-facing output.

## Airstrip
- Coordinate every flight day/time with יונתן.
- Identify other people expected at the airstrip.
- If they are workers, require a safety briefing and active movement control.
- If they are another company, flag exposure, frequency, communication, and mutual interference risks.
- If departure is after dark, add a WhatsApp task for שמעון with the planned departure time.

## Fuel (`דס״ל`)
- Estimate need from the flight profile: light/cart activity, heavy platform, long-range flight, high-consumption profile, or routine/local flights.
- Use the truck with the jet-fuel tank for `דס״ל` transport and refill drives.
- Do not substitute the other `דימקס` when it is configured as the diesel tank.
- The jet-fuel tank holds up to 320 liters.
- If the truck does not leave full, compare starting liters to expected flights and decide whether a refill drive may be needed.
- Refill drives take meaningful time; add route, timing, truck, driver/commander, and notes to `Vehicles/Drives`.
- Load black jerrycans when tarmac refueling may need to happen away from the truck.
- If shortage or midweek refill is likely, plan a `שדה תימן` refill drive with the jet-fuel tank truck.
- Coordinate `שדה תימן` refueling with סתיו and refuel in multiples of 20 liters.
- Mix `דס״ל` with dedicated oil at 1:20 and verify uniform blending.
- Unknown quantity, truck identity, jerrycan count, or oil availability becomes an assumption or blocker by risk.

## TRR, Lodging, And Rentals
- After TRR, use experiment content and staffing to count attendees and צימר sleepers.
- Default lodging key is two people per room/suite unless data or the PM says otherwise.
- If people rotate through one room, notify the צימר owner and request linen replacement.
- Plan vehicles by origin hub: Tel Aviv and Yokneam.
- Rentals use the שלמה סיקסט online form and pickup at רחוב המסגר in Tel Aviv or חיפה.
- Add rentals to כביש 6 and remove them on the return date.
- Check mileage limits against the planned week and compare extra rental days to mileage penalties.

## Equipment And Trucking
- Derive critical equipment from experiment content: antennas, concrete blocks, specific vehicles, routers, screens, air conditioning, tools, and special items.
- Add critical equipment to the standing equipment list and capture loading vehicle when possible.
- If critical equipment has an arrival time, cross-check vehicle, driver, and expected arrival.
- Decide truck count from platform/asset and trailer counts.
- A truck can carry two platforms/assets plus one trailer, or two trailers plus one platform/asset.
- A mini crate may be added for each pair of platforms/assets when relevant.

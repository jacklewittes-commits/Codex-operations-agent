# PM Review Checklist

After generating the Google Sheet, keep the PM review focused on the live Google Sheet itself rather than local files or internal payloads.

## Always Review
- `איושים`: attendance, required roles, loading staff, and plausible daily totals.
- `מעודכן שיבוצי רכבים`: outbound, return, internal drives, commanders, passengers, rentals, and truck rows.
- `שיבוצי לינה`: every overnight attendee has a valid room and night assignment.
- `הזמנות אוכל`: headcounts, standard meals, special food counts, vendors, and order channels.
- Hidden/internal PM checklist output, when present: all open operational tasks are visible with owner, timing, status, and blockers.

## Domain Review
Use `references/pm_review_items.md` for detailed airstrip, fuel, rental, equipment, lodging, trucking, and checklist review prompts.

## Flags
- Resolve all blocking flags before publishing the plan to the team.
- Non-blocking assumptions can remain only if the PM accepts them.
- Items already completed are marked `Done` only when the PM explicitly confirmed completion.

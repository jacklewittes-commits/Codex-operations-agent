## Quantity model

- Historical lunches intentionally provide variety and buffer; do not size only one portion per person.
- For non-vegetarian protein portions:
  - 11-15 people: 15-20 pieces when using one piece protein.
  - 16-20 people: 20-25 pieces.
  - 21-25 people: 25-30 pieces.
  - If using two piece proteins, combined pieces commonly land around 2 portions per non-vegetarian. Use the lower end when also ordering a pot/tray main.
- For tray/pot mains:
  - `קציצות בקר`: 1 tray for small groups when secondary, 2 trays for 14-25 people when it is a main choice.
  - `סיר מפרום`: 1 pot for 16-18 people when secondary, 2 pots when primary or when headcount is around 19+.
  - `דגים מרוקאיים`: if ordered as trays, one tray is 5 portions; if quoted per person, order actual portions.
- For carbs and sides:
  - `אורז/תפו"א/פסטה ברוטב אדום`: 1 tray is enough up to at least 22 when paired with another carb/side.
  - `קוסקוס`: add 1 tray for 14+ people, or when serving `סיר מפרום`/`קציצות בקר`.
  - `לביבות תפו"א`, `מוקפץ איטריות אורז עוף/טופו`, `פדתאי תאילנדי עוף`, or `סיר ממולאים`: usually 1 tray/pot as a side or special-diet support.
  - `סלט ירקות`: 1 tray for up to 16 if there are other sides; 2 trays for 17+ or when one tray needs to be without onion.

## Vegetarian and special diets

- Before finalizing, read `data/food_specials.csv` and compare the special counts against the PM context. If the PM text conflicts with the CSV, flag the mismatch.
- Aroma lunch default: use one `Aroma salad` row per person whose lunch is handled by Aroma. Do not ask for exact salad type unless the PM volunteers it.
- Do not replace all catering sides for vegetarians; shared carbs and `סלט ירקות` still count toward total headcount.
- For gluten-free needs, prefer explicit item notes such as `ללא גלוטן` and avoid relying on `שניצלים`.

## Flexible ordering

The catering CSV is a baseline menu, not a locked menu. The PM may request other food by WhatsApp, email, phone, or manual coordination. When that happens, keep the item in the food order tab with its channel and mark unknown prices or vendor confirmations as review flags.
